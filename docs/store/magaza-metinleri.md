Teknik direktörlük koltuğu seni bekliyor! Süper Menajer'de 13 ligden dilediğin kulübü seç, kadronu kur,
taktiğini çiz ve maçları 2D sahada canlı izle.

## English

**App name:** Super Menajer: Football Manager

**Short description:**
Run your club, set your tactics, watch matches live. Offline, no ads.

**Full description:**

Take the manager's seat! Pick any club from 13 leagues, build your squad, design your tactics and watch every
match live on a 2D pitch.

⚽ Live match engine — every attack is played out; change mentality and make subs in real time.
📋 Deep tactics — drag-and-drop board, player roles, team instructions, opponent-specific preparation.
💼 Transfers & club management — negotiations, loans, release clauses, pre-contracts, budget, sponsors,
stadium, facilities and youth academy.
🏆 Career — league, cups, European competitions, national team job, board objectives, press conferences.
👥 Two-player mode on one phone.
🔋 Fully offline, no ads, three save slots with automatic backups, battery saver mode.

Club and player names in the game are fictional.
